What is Extremism in Politics?

Extremism is sometimes hard to see and is often masked in language that makes it seem like the obvious solution. A lack of majority support does not make a position extremist, but if it meets any of these criteria, it often is:

This is How We Define it

  • Policy positions far outside public support, that are used to agitate a vocal minority
  • Often born of small or infrequent events blown out of proportion
  • Usually fear based
  • Often demonizes an entire group and promotes zero sum governance (no win-win solutions)
  • Can be destructive of established institutions or norms
